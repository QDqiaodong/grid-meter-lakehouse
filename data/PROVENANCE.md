# Data provenance

All bundled records are original synthetic examples created for this project.
They describe fictional entities and are not measurements, customers, service
history, official standards, or records copied from another project.
They are distributed under the repository MIT license. No external dataset or
third-party source code is vendored. Dependencies retain their own licenses.

## Tariff plans

`data/tariffs/01-peak-aware.json` and `data/tariffs/02-flat-saver.json` are
original synthetic examples invented for the monthly comparison tests.
Segment times, tier thresholds, demand prices and fixed fees are illustrative
only: they are not any real utility's tariff, published schedule, or
regulatory rule, and they describe no real customer or billed amount.
